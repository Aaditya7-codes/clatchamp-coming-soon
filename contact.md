CLAT Champ is operated by **Aaditya Sharma, trading as CLAT Champ**. We answer by email.

## Email

| For | Write to |
|---|---|
| Your account, sign-in problems, or something not working | [support@clatchamp.com](mailto:support@clatchamp.com) |
| Premium payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds | [support@clatchamp.com](mailto:support@clatchamp.com) |
| Questions about the exam, the site or partnerships | [contact@clatchamp.com](mailto:contact@clatchamp.com) |
| Privacy, or deleting your data | [privacy@clatchamp.com](mailto:privacy@clatchamp.com) |

When you write about your account or a payment, use the email address you signed in with and include the date and amount of any payment. That lets us find it quickly.

Premium is a one-time pass with no automatic renewal, so there is nothing to cancel. You can see when your Premium ends under **Account** at [clatchamp.com/app](/app/). The [cancellation and refunds](/refunds/) page explains refunds.
