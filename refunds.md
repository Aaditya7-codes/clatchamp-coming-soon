## What you are buying

CLAT CHAMP Premium on [clatchamp.com/app](/app/) unlocks all practice sets and full-length mock tests in the web version while your subscription is active. It is a digital service. Nothing is shipped; access starts as soon as your payment is confirmed, usually within a minute.

| Plan | Price | Billed |

| 3 months | ₹999 | every 3 months |
| 1 year | ₹1,999 | every year |

Prices include any applicable taxes. Payments are processed by Razorpay using UPI, cards or net banking. We do not receive or store your card or UPI details.

## Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel. Your bank or UPI app may ask you to approve the recurring mandate when you first subscribe.

## Cancelling

Sign in at [clatchamp.com/app](/app/), open **Settings → Manage subscription**, and choose **Cancel automatic renewal**. You can also email [support@clatchamp.com](mailto:support@clatchamp.com) from the address you signed in with. Cancelling stops future charges. Premium stays available until the end of the period you have already paid for.

## Refunds

Because Premium content is available immediately, we do not refund the unused part of a period after you cancel. We will refund in full if:

- you were charged twice for the same period;
- you were charged after cancelling renewal; or
- a fault on our side stopped you using Premium and we could not fix it within a reasonable time.

Email [support@clatchamp.com](mailto:support@clatchamp.com) within 7 days of the charge, from the address you signed in with, with the payment date and amount. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days of approval, depending on your bank. Nothing here limits any right you have under applicable law.

## Purchases made in the iPhone app

Subscriptions bought in the CLAT CHAMP iPhone app are sold and billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them in your Apple subscription settings, and request refunds from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot cancel or refund Apple purchases.
