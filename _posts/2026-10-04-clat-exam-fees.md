**The CLAT 2027 UG application fee is ₹4,000 for General and OBC candidates, and ₹3,500 for SC, ST, BPL and PwD candidates.** It is paid online while submitting the application, which closes on **31 October 2026 at 11:59 p.m.**

## The fee by category

| Category | Application fee |
|---|---|
| General, OBC | **₹4,000** |
| SC, ST, BPL, PwD | **₹3,500** |

To use the lower fee you must belong to one of those categories and have the matching certificate ready to upload with the form.

## What is extra

The Consortium publishes **past years' question papers at ₹500**. This is separate from the application fee and optional. You do not need to buy it to apply.

Bank or payment-gateway charges are **borne by the candidate**, so the amount leaving your account may be slightly more than the fee shown.

## What you cannot get back

The instructions describe the fee as **non-refundable and non-adjustable**. That means:

- It is not returned if you change your mind or miss the exam.
- It cannot be carried over to another year or another payment.

## If you apply more than once

The instructions say that if a candidate submits **multiple applications, only the latest is considered**, and the fees paid earlier are not refunded. Submit one complete application rather than starting again.

## How to pay

Payment is **online only**, as one step in the application. The full sequence is in our [CLAT registration guide](/blog/clat-registration/). Keep the payment confirmation and a PDF of the submitted form.

Do not leave payment to the last evening. A failed transaction near the deadline is hard to fix, and the instructions allow no updates after applications close.

## What the fee does not cover

The application fee is for taking the CLAT. Costs after the result, such as admission and counselling charges, are set by the Consortium and the individual universities and are announced separately. This page does not list them.
