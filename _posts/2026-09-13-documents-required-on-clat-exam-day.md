Carry two things without fail: your **admit card** and a **valid photo identity document**. Everything else on this page is detail around those two.

## The checklist

**Must carry**

- **Admit card**, printed. Not a screenshot on a phone — phones are not permitted in the hall.
- **A valid photo ID** in original: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence, voter ID, or school identity card with photograph.
- **Blue or black ballpoint pens.** Carry two. The OMR sheet is marked by hand.
- **Category certificate**, if your admit card or the current year's instructions require it. Check yours rather than assuming.

**Usually permitted**

- A transparent water bottle
- An analogue watch — check the current year's instructions, as rules on watches have tightened at many exams
- A simple clipboard or exam pad, if your admit card says one is needed

**Not permitted**

- Mobile phones, smart watches, fitness bands, earphones, any electronic device
- Calculators of any kind
- Books, notes, rough paper of your own
- Bags, pouches, wallets beyond what the centre allows
- Food, except where a medical condition has been declared and approved in advance

## Before you leave

**Print the admit card more than once.** Keep a spare in your bag. Printers fail on the morning of exams with a reliability that feels deliberate.

**Check the photograph and details on the admit card** against your ID. A mismatch is easier to resolve a week beforehand than at the gate.

**Locate the centre in advance.** If you can, travel to it once. Reporting time is usually well before the 2:00 p.m. start, and a centre you have never seen on a Sunday afternoon is not the place to discover a road closure.

## If something is missing

If you arrive without your admit card, speak to the centre superintendent immediately rather than joining the queue and hoping. Do not attempt to enter with a phone in your pocket on the assumption nobody will check — possession of a prohibited device can invalidate your paper entirely.

## The night before

Put the admit card, ID and pens in one place by the door. Then stop studying. [The paper is comprehension-led](/blog/clat-exam-pattern/) and comprehension is the first thing sleep deprivation takes.

## Where this comes from

Exact rules on permitted items are set in the Consortium's instructions and on the admit card for each cycle, and they change. **Your admit card is the authority** — read it fully, and treat this page as a prompt rather than a substitute.
