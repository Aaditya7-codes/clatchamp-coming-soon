**There are about nine weeks between 5 October and the exam on Sunday, 6 December 2026. Use them in four stages: diagnose, repair, build stamina, then protect what you have.** This is an illustrative plan, not an official schedule. Scale the hours to your day and swap in your own weak spots.

## The plan at a glance

| Weeks | Dates | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5–11 Oct | Diagnose: take a full mock, build an error log |
| 2–3 | 12–25 Oct | Repair your weakest two sections; keep a daily reading habit |
| 4 | 26 Oct–1 Nov | Finish the application **before 31 Oct**, then mock rhythm |
| 5–6 | 2–15 Nov | Full mocks and sectional tests; fix repeat errors |
| 7–8 | 16–29 Nov | Full mocks in the real slot; revise notes and current affairs |
| 9 | 30 Nov–6 Dec | Light revision, rest, exam logistics |

## Week 1: diagnose

Take one full-length, 120-minute mock and review every error using our [mock analysis guide](/blog/how-to-analyse-a-clat-mock-test/). Note accuracy and time by section. Decide your two weakest sections.

## Weeks 2–3: repair

Spend most of your study time on the weakest two sections, using short daily sets rather than a long weekly one. Keep a daily passage of reading. Start current affairs notes now and update them weekly. See [how to prepare current affairs for CLAT](/blog/how-to-prepare-current-affairs-for-clat/).

## Week 4: the form, then rhythm

**Applications close on 31 October at 11:59 p.m.** Complete and pay well before the last evening. See the [registration guide](/blog/clat-registration/). Then settle into a mock rhythm.

## Weeks 5–6: stamina

Take full mocks and sectional tests. After each, spend as long on the review as on the test, and fix errors that repeat. Compare your scores across several mocks, not one. See [how many mock tests to take](/blog/how-many-mock-tests-for-clat/).

## Weeks 7–8: exam conditions

Take full mocks between 2:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m., the real slot, with the same breaks and stationery you plan to use. Practise your time budget from the [time management guide](/blog/clat-time-management/). Revise your error log and current affairs notes.

## Week 9: protect, do not cram

No new topics in the final days. One light mock early in the week at most. Check your admit card, centre and journey, sleep on a normal schedule, and pack your documents the evening before. See [CLAT exam day](/blog/clat-exam-day/).

## If you have fewer than 60 days or fewer hours

Keep the order: diagnose, repair the weakest section, take mocks, rest. Drop the extras first, not the mocks or the review.
