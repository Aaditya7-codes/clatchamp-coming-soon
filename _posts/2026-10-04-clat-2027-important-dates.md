**CLAT 2027 UG is on Sunday, 6 December 2026, from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. Applications close on 31 October 2026 at 11:59 p.m.**

## Confirmed dates

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Registration opens | **3 August 2026** |
| Registration closes | **31 October 2026**, 11:59 p.m. |
| Exam date | **Sunday, 6 December 2026** |
| Exam timing | **2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.** |
| Timing for eligible PwD candidates | 2:00 p.m. to 4:40 p.m. |

## Not announced yet

As of 4 October 2026 the official instructions give no date for these. Do not trust a date from a social post or a coaching page until you see it on the Consortium's site.

- **Admit card release.** The instructions say the date will be announced on the website. See [CLAT 2027 admit card](/blog/clat-2027-admit-card/).
- **Result.** See [when will the CLAT result be declared](/blog/when-will-clat-result-be-declared/).
- **Counselling.** See the [counselling process](/blog/clat-counselling-process/).

## What to do before each date

- **Before 31 October:** complete the form, upload documents and pay the fee. The [registration guide](/blog/clat-registration/) lists the steps and the [fees page](/blog/clat-exam-fees/) lists the amounts.
- **Before the exam:** take full-length mocks in the real 2-hour slot. See the [last 60 days plan](/blog/clat-study-plan-last-60-days/).
- **When the admit card is out:** check every detail on it against your documents.
