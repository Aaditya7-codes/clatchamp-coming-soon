**The CLAT 2027 admit card date has not been published.** The official instructions say it will be announced on the Consortium's website. We checked on 4 October 2026 and found no date.

## What we know

- The exam is on **Sunday, 6 December 2026**, from **2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.** Eligible PwD candidates have until 4:40 p.m.
- Applications close on **31 October 2026**, so completing the form and payment before then is the first requirement.
- The admit card, not a coaching page or a message in a group, is your authority for your centre and reporting details.

## Check these the moment it is released

- **Name, parents' names and date of birth.** Compare each with your certificates. The instructions stress that names must be spelt correctly.
- **Photograph and signature.**
- **Exam centre and address.** Plan the journey before the exam week.
- **Reporting time and gate-closing time.** The exam start time is not your arrival deadline.
- **The list of permitted and prohibited items.**
- **Category and PwD details**, if you claimed them.

If anything looks wrong, use the contact details on the official Consortium site straight away rather than waiting for the exam day.

## Prepare before it arrives

- Keep your registration email, mobile number and password safe. You registered with them, and you will probably need them to sign in.
- Arrange a printer or a print shop, and keep a spare copy.
- Read our [exam day documents checklist](/blog/documents-required-on-clat-exam-day/) so nothing surprises you.

## Beware of fake notices

Only trust messages that lead to the official Consortium website. A "download admit card" link from an unknown sender is a reason to stop and type the address yourself.
