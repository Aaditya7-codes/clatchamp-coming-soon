By reading the site you accept the terms below. They are short, and written to
be understood rather than to be impressive.

## We are not an official source

We write about CLAT. We do not run it, and we have no influence over it.

Where we state a fact — a date, a fee, an eligibility threshold — we take it
from the Consortium's own notification and show the date we last checked it.
Those checks age. **Rules change between cycles, and the Consortium's
notification always overrides anything you read here.** Before you act on a
deadline, a fee or an eligibility rule, confirm it at the source. We link to it
for exactly that reason.

## Information, not advice

The guides on this site are general information for a general audience. They are
not legal advice, admissions counselling, or a personal recommendation about
what you specifically should do.

We do not predict results, forecast cut-offs, rank colleges, or promise
outcomes. We do not publish figures we cannot verify — which is why you will not
find a rank predictor or a table of counselling fees here, even though people
search for both.

CLAT CHAMP is a practice app that runs in your browser at
[clatchamp.com/app](/app/). Some practice is free. The rest needs a Premium
subscription. Practice questions are original exam-style material written for
preparation; they are not past papers and do not predict your CLAT score or admission.
