**CLAT Champ is a free, independent website that explains the CLAT exam.** It is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Consortium of National Law
Universities, any National Law University, or any other examination or
government body. Nothing here is official, and nothing here is for sale.

By reading the site you accept the terms below. They are short, and written to
be understood rather than to be impressive.

## We are not an official source

We write about CLAT. We do not run it, and we have no influence over it.

Where we state a fact — a date, a fee, an eligibility threshold — we take it
from the Consortium's own notification and show the date we last checked it.
Those checks age. **Rules change between cycles, and the Consortium's
notification always overrides anything you read here.** Before you act on a
deadline, a fee or an eligibility rule, confirm it at the source. We link to it
for exactly that reason.

## Information, not advice

The guides on this site are general information for a general audience. They are
not legal advice, admissions counselling, or a personal recommendation about
what you specifically should do.

We do not predict results, forecast cut-offs, rank colleges, or promise
outcomes. We do not publish figures we cannot verify — which is why you will not
find a rank predictor or a table of counselling fees here, even though people
search for both.

An app is in development. It has not been released, we have not announced a
launch date, and there is nothing on this site to download, join, reserve or pay
for. If you see a claim otherwise anywhere else, it did not come from us.

These terms cover this website and blog only. If and when the app ships, it will
have its own terms and its own privacy policy.

## Using what is here

passages with credit and a link back.

What you may not do: republish whole guides elsewhere, present our writing as
your own, sell it, or scrape the site in bulk or by automated means. The
writing, design, logo and name are ours and stay ours.

We work hard to be accurate, and we correct errors when they are pointed out.

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from decisions

writing; it is why we tell you to confirm anything that matters against the

## Found a mistake?

Tell us. Write to **[contact@clatchamp.com](mailto:contact@clatchamp.com)** with

This matters more to us than it might sound. A single wrong eligibility figure
