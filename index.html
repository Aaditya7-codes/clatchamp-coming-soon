Questions you might have

Are these real CLAT questions?

No. Every question is original, written in the style of recent CLAT papers. CLAT Champ is independent and not affiliated with the Consortium of National Law Universities. For the official pattern, see our guide to the CLAT exam pattern.

What do I need?

A laptop or desktop with an up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. It also opens on tablets and phones, but mocks are easier on a bigger screen.

Do I need an account?

No. You can practise for free without one, and your progress is kept in the browser you use. Free accounts that carry your progress to any computer are coming soon.

How are the mocks marked?

Like CLAT: 120 questions in 120 minutes, +1 for a correct answer, −0.25 for a wrong answer and 0 for an unanswered question.

Are there ads, or is my child’s data sold?

No. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics and no selling of data. Study data is used only to run practice and show progress. See the Privacy Policy and our refund policy.
