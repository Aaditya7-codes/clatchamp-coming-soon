How it works

A plan you can keep for the months before CLAT.

  1. 1

    Find your reading speed

    Read one short passage at your natural pace and answer five questions. You get your words per minute and accuracy as a starting point.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Test yourself like it’s the real thing

    Sit full-length mocks: 120 questions, 120 minutes, +1 and −0.25. Then review every answer and revisit your mistakes.

Questions

Good to know.

Is CLAT Champ free?

Yes. Question of the Day, one complete set in each of the five sections and full-length Mock 1 are free, with explanations and progress tracking. Premium adds the full question bank and all 10 mocks.

What do I need to use CLAT Champ?

A laptop or desktop computer with an up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. There’s nothing to install, and free practice needs no account. A free account, with your email or Google, saves your progress so you can continue on any computer; you need one to subscribe to Premium. It opens on tablets and phones too, but sets and mocks are designed for a bigger screen.

Are these real CLAT questions?

No. Every question is original, written in the style of recent CLAT papers. CLAT Champ is independent and not affiliated with the Consortium of National Law Universities. For the official pattern, see our guide to the CLAT exam pattern.

How are the mocks marked?

Like CLAT: 120 questions in 120 minutes, +1 for a correct answer, −0.25 for a wrong answer and 0 for an unanswered question. You can move between sections, flag questions and clear answers.

Where is my progress saved?

Without an account, in your browser on that computer only. With a free account, it’s also saved to your account, so your streak and history follow you to any computer where you sign in. You can delete your account and its data at any time.
